2026

ML research

Scale250 cross-modal representation study

Investigated whether language and vision models converge on similar concept representations.

  • Tested 25 language, vision, and multimodal models on 250 concepts from three image sources.
  • Showed that a small benchmark made language and vision look more similar than they were; expanding from 20 to 250 concepts reduced language-image RSA from 0.2418 to 0.0199 on the same model panel.
  • Used bootstrap confidence intervals, 3,000-permutation Mantel tests, FDR correction, prompt controls, depth analysis, and CKA.
  • Published the manuscript, code, manifests, checksums, reconstruction scripts, and reproducibility boundaries.
