How I build

Models sit inside systems.

The model is rarely the whole product. The consequential work is deciding what context it receives, what it may change, how output is tested, and what happens when confidence runs out.

  • Keep model output reviewable Receipt extraction remains editable, and every Nepal Energy Wiki claim retains a visible path back to its source.
  • Turn ambiguity into structure Receipts become bill items, reports become facts and map layers, and embeddings become comparable concept geometries.
  • Test the claim, not the demo Search benchmarks, statistical controls, release checks, and production repair work expose where a system actually holds.
  • Design a path through failure Circuit breakers, manual entry, bounded agent sessions, and explicit evidence gaps make failure recoverable rather than surprising.
