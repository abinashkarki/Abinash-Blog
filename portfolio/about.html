How I work

A few principles that recur.

  • Start from the real problem. Architecture follows the actual bottleneck, not the most impressive model choice.
  • Keep AI output inspectable. Receipts can be corrected, claims link back to sources, and experiments keep their assumptions visible.
  • Prefer systems one person can repair. Clear ownership, explicit data models, and tests matter more than a larger stack.
  • Let evidence change the design. User behavior, audits, failures, and stronger measurements should be allowed to change the story.
